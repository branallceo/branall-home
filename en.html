VIRAL DISTRIBUTION

Content placed where Korean users already scroll

Instagram community pages, YouTube Shorts channels and large X accounts. We own or directly manage the channels, so schedule and reporting stay in one place.

INFLUENCER SEEDING

Product reviews from accounts that fit the brand

Instagram, X, Naver blogs and Xiaohongshu. We shortlist creators, ship products, check each post and report the results.
